How SkillSwap Works

No payments, no hidden fees. Just peer-to-peer knowledge sharing between college students.

01

Create Your Profile

List the skills you are strong at and willing to teach, alongside the skills you want to learn this semester.

02

Discover Skill Partners

Filter by department, year, or skill categories to find fellow students whose learning goals complement yours.

03

Swap & Collaborate

Send a swap request, schedule study sessions in the campus library or online, and elevate your skills together.

Why SkillSwap?

Built For Collaborative Learning

Empowering students to overcome expensive tutorials through direct peer-to-peer mentorship.

100% Free Knowledge Exchange

No subscriptions or paid courses. A fair barter system where students trade their knowledge and skills with each other.

Verified Campus Network

Connect with peers using institutional college emails. Build trusted connections within your university or department.

Practical Portfolio Building

Learn real practical skills by working on mini-projects together, rather than passively watching video playlists.

Skill Matching Algorithm

Complementary matching identifies students whose teaching strengths align with your personal learning goals.

Flexible Study Schedules

Coordinate sessions around semester timetables, assignment deadlines, and exam preparations on your terms.

Peer Endorsements & Ratings

Gain credibility and verified ratings from fellow students for the skills you effectively teach.
